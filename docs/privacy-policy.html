Privacy Policy for CouponKeeper

Effective date: November 3, 2026

In short: CouponKeeper stores everything you enter — promo codes, channels, and your app settings — only on your own device, and never sends them anywhere. The app has no account system. The only data that leaves your device is what is needed for the optional Premium purchase: an anonymous identifier and your purchase details, which go to Google Play and to RevenueCat, the service that processes in-app purchases.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the CouponKeeper mobile app (Android; iOS is not yet published), developed as an independent project. It explains what data the app handles and how.

2. What data the app stores, and where

CouponKeeper is a local-first app. The promo codes you create (code, discount, validity dates, usage limit and count, an optional note, and the sales channel you assign to it), the channels you define, and your app preferences (theme, default sort order, and your expiry-reminder threshold) are stored in a SQLite database on your device only.

  • No user account, sign-in, or registration exists in the app.
  • Your promo codes, channels, notes and settings are never uploaded to any server, and are never shared with, or sold to, any third party.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Cloud backup / cross-device sync is not currently offered. If that changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the feature ships, and it will be opt-in.

Premium users can create a backup file of their channels and promo codes, or export their promo codes to a CSV file. These files are created only when you ask for them, and the app hands them to your device’s share menu: they go only where you choose to save or send them (for example your own Google Drive or email). The app never uploads them anywhere by itself, and the developer never receives them. Restoring from a backup reads a file you pick on your device.

3. In-app purchases

CouponKeeper offers an optional one-time Premium purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the app and the developer never see your payment details. To confirm a purchase and let you restore it later, the app uses RevenueCat, a third-party in-app purchase service, and the following data is sent to it:

  • a random anonymous identifier created by the app, which is not linked to your name or email address;
  • your purchase history for this app (which product, when, and the store's purchase token);
  • basic technical details such as the app version, device platform and country or store region.

The app contacts Google Play and RevenueCat when it starts, when you return to it, and when you buy or restore, including if you never buy anything. Your purchase is also subject to Google's own privacy policy. This data is used only to provide and verify the purchase, not for advertising.

4. Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only to show local, on-device reminders when a coupon you created is approaching its expiry date (the threshold is configurable in Settings). These reminders are scheduled and shown entirely by your device's operating system — nothing about your coupons is transmitted anywhere to generate them, and you can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device's app settings without losing any other functionality.
  • Run at startup, vibration and wake lock (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCK): added by the notification component so scheduled reminders survive a device restart and can buzz when they arrive. They serve only the local reminders described above.
  • Internet: used only for the in-app purchase described in section 3. Your promo codes never use it, and every free feature works with no network.
  • Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): lets the app show Google Play's purchase screen. It runs only when you start a purchase or restore one.

5. Data deletion

Your promo codes live only on your device, so you are always in full control of them:

  • Delete an individual coupon or channel at any time from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your device's app settings, permanently deletes all of its data from your device.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat so that your purchase can be restored. To ask for the RevenueCat record linked to your anonymous identifier to be deleted, write to the address in section 8. Deleting it may mean the purchase has to be restored again.

6. Children's privacy

CouponKeeper is a general-audience productivity tool for makers and sellers managing their own promotions, not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if an opt-in cloud backup feature is added in the future — this page will be updated and the "Effective date" above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
